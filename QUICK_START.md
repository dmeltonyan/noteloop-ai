## 🎯 **Feature Details**

- Tracks when concepts need review
- Automatic scheduling based on mastery
- Shows due items in feed
- API: `/spaced-repetition/due`, `/spaced-repetition/review`

### **2. Flashcard Mode**
- Swipeable cards (flip with click or Space)
- Keyboard navigation (←/→)
- Hint support
- Progress tracking
- Know It / Don't Know buttons

### **3. Achievement System**
- 10 achievements automatically unlock
- Badge gallery with filters
- Achievement notifications on unlock
- Progress tracking
- Categories: streak, mastery, practice

### **4. Study Streaks**
