# 🎁 Reward System Design Suggestions

## 💰 **Credit System Options**

### Option 1: **Learning Credits (LC)** - Simple & Effective ⭐⭐⭐
**Concept**: Users earn credits for learning activities, can redeem for:
- **Premium features** (unlimited uploads, advanced analytics)
- **AI tutor sessions** (extra personalized help)
- **Study materials** (access to premium content library)
- **Badges/achievements** (cosmetic rewards)
- **Future marketplace items** (when you add a store)

**Earning Rates**:
- Answer question correctly: +10 LC
- Complete study session (10+ questions): +50 LC
- Master a concept: +100 LC
- Daily streak bonus: +25 LC per day
- Upload document: +200 LC
- Review due concepts: +15 LC per review

**Redemption Examples**:
- 500 LC = 1 premium AI tutor session
- 1000 LC = Unlock advanced analytics for 1 month
- 2000 LC = Unlimited uploads for 1 month
- 5000 LC = Access to premium study materials library

---

### Option 2: **Study Coins** - Gamified Currency ⭐⭐⭐
**Concept**: More gamified, can be used for:
- **Power-ups** (hints, extra time, skip question)
- **Customization** (themes, avatars, backgrounds)
- **Unlock features** (new study modes, advanced tools)
- **Leaderboard boosts** (temporary ranking multipliers)

**Earning**:
- Base: 5 coins per question answered
- Streak multiplier: 1.5x for 3+ day streak
- Perfect session bonus: +100 coins
- Weekly challenges: +500 coins

**Spending**:
- 50 coins = 1 hint
- 100 coins = Skip difficult question
- 200 coins = Unlock new theme
- 500 coins = 1 week premium features

---

### Option 3: **Knowledge Points (KP)** - Educational Focus ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Concept**: Points that unlock educational value:
- **Advanced explanations** (detailed AI breakdowns)
- **Practice exams** (full-length tests)
- **Study guides** (AI-generated summaries)
- **Video explanations** (visual learning aids)
- **Concept deep-dives** (extended learning)

**Earning**:
- Learning activities earn KP
- Bonus for consistent study
- Bonus for mastering difficult concepts

**Redemption**:
- 100 KP = Generate custom study guide
- 200 KP = Create practice exam
- 300 KP = Get video explanation
- 500 KP = Unlock concept deep-dive

---

### Option 4: **Hybrid System** - Best of All Worlds ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Concept**: Multiple currencies for different purposes:

1. **Learning Credits (LC)** - For premium features
2. **Study Coins (SC)** - For power-ups and customization
3. **Knowledge Points (KP)** - For educational content

**Benefits**:
- Clear separation of reward types
- More engaging (multiple progress bars)
- Flexible redemption options

---

### **Performance Bonuses**
- Perfect session (100% correct): +200 LC

## 💡 **Recommended System: Hybrid Approach**

### **Primary Currency: Learning Credits (LC)**
- Main reward currency
- Earned from all learning activities
- Redeemable for premium features

### **Secondary Currency: Study Coins (SC)**
- Gamified currency
- Earned from streaks and challenges
- Used for power-ups and customization

### **Tertiary Currency: Knowledge Points (KP)**
- Educational currency
- Earned from mastering concepts
- Used for educational content generation

## 🎮 **Gamification Elements**

### **Levels & Progression**
- Level up by earning LC
- Each level unlocks new features
- Visual progress bar

### **Badges & Achievements**
- Convert to visual badges
- Showcase on profile
- Unlock special rewards

### **Leaderboards**
- Weekly/monthly rankings
- Rewards for top performers
- Friendly competition

### **Challenges**
- Daily challenges: +100 LC
- Weekly challenges: +500 LC
- Monthly challenges: +2000 LC

---

## 📊 **Implementation Strategy**

### **Phase 1: Core System**
- Learning Credits (LC) only
- Basic earning mechanics
- Simple redemption (premium features)

### **Phase 2: Gamification**
- Add Study Coins (SC)
- Power-ups and customization
- Badges and levels

### **Phase 3: Advanced**
- Knowledge Points (KP)
- Marketplace integration
- Partnership rewards

---

## 🎯 **Key Principles**

1. **Immediate Gratification**: Small rewards for daily actions
2. **Long-term Goals**: Big rewards for milestones
3. **Clear Value**: Users understand what credits are worth
4. **Fair Earning**: Credits earned through learning, not just time
5. **Meaningful Rewards**: Items that enhance learning experience
